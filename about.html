ABOUT THE ENGINE

What Is NECSTORY?

A dark story engine for storytellers, creators & the endlessly curious.

The Mission

NECSTORY gathers ghost stories, true-crime cases, dark-web tales, unsolved mysteries, razor-sharp horror seeds and public-domain Hindi classics — in one place, with zero login and zero paywall. Every story opens inside this website: tap a card and the full text appears in the in-app reader. Nothing redirects you away.

How It Works

Story cards are summoned live from public APIs — Wikipedia (Hindi + English) and the Creepypasta Wiki — plus two datasets bundled with the site: 2,000 two-sentence horror seeds and 53 full-length Hindi classics. Search any realm, or hit Surprise Me and let the darkness choose.

  • GitHub datasets — horror seeds (Apache-2.0) & Hindi classics (MIT).

Accuracy Labels

Not every dark tale is equally true. Read the badges honestly:

  • VERIFIED — documented incident with reliable sources.
  • ALLEGED — claimed account; evidence disputed or thin.
  • LEGEND — folklore passed down through generations.
  • FICTION — written as horror fiction (e.g. Creepypasta).
